# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Readers visit for Andrew's writing. Potential employers and collaborators visit to understand his experience and how he thinks and works. The site should serve both groups equally.

## Product Purpose

Give Andrew a personal home for his writing and professional identity. Help visitors find his posts, understand his work, and reach his professional profiles or résumé.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, fun, engaging, sophisticated, direct, and competent.

## Anti-references

Avoid fluff, unnecessary decoration, and generic SaaS styling.

## Design Principles

- Make writing and professional experience equally easy to discover.
- Be direct and let real work and ideas demonstrate competence.
- Keep the experience minimal without making it dull.
- Use personality in service of content and navigation, not decoration for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, keyboard access, and reduced-motion support across light and dark themes.
