# Personal Correspondence

The site should feel like a thoughtful note from Andrew: direct, warm, and confident. Writing and professional experience should be equally easy to find. Keep the personality in the typography and small details, not extra content.

## Color

- Light: white background `#ffffff`, ink `#0f172a`, secondary text `#475569`, rule `#cbd5e1`, accent `#d4320a`.
- Dark: background `#0a0a0a`, ink `#f1f5f9`, secondary text `#cbd5e1`, rule `#404040`, accent `#ff9a7f`.
- Use the accent sparingly for emphasis and hover states; never rely on color alone to signal a link or focus.

## Typography and layout

- Georgia and its system-serif fallbacks give page titles a personal, letter-like voice. DM Sans remains the readable body and interface face.
- Keep body copy comfortably narrow, with generous paragraph spacing. Give headings room without making navigation or reading feel distant.
- The homepage lists posts directly, without an introduction or section heading. Post pages put the title and date before the writing. The résumé stays easy to scan and print.

## Components and interaction

- The homepage has no banner or introduction. The paper, bird, and Type Collector banner explorations were rejected; do not restore them.
- Post links should read as a list, with a clear title, date, and separate tags. Links remain visibly identifiable before hover.
- Keep navigation and controls legible in both themes. All interactive elements need visible keyboard focus.
- Respect reduced-motion settings. Preserve the résumé's print and PDF layout.

## Avoid

Generic SaaS cards, striped page borders, unnecessary imagery, excess animation, and invented professional facts.
